What is an LED?

An LED is a special light that is used in many devices around us! It stands for Light Emitting Diode. LEDs are small but powerful lights that use less electricity and last a long time. They're used in robots, toys, traffic lights, and even TV screens!

How Does an LED Work?

When electricity flows through an LED, it passes through a special material that creates light. This process is called electroluminescence! LEDs have a positive side (anode) and a negative side (cathode), so electricity must flow in the correct direction.

Fun Facts About LEDs

  • LEDs last up to 50,000 hours! That's almost 6 years of continuous use.
  • They come in many colors: red, green, blue, and even ultraviolet and infrared!
  • LEDs are energy-efficient and save power compared to traditional bulbs.
  • The first practical LED was invented in 1962 by Nick Holonyak Jr.
  • LEDs are used in NASA's space missions because they're reliable and efficient.

LED Color Mixing Activity

Try mixing different colored LEDs to create new colors! Here's how:

  • Red + Green = Yellow
  • Red + Blue = Magenta
  • Green + Blue = Cyan
  • Red + Green + Blue = White
